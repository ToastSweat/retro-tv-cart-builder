RETRO TV CART BUILDER v1.5.1
==============================

AUTO-PLAY / PREVIEW UX FIX

v1.5.0 only selected a clip when you clicked it. The video element was
deliberately cleared and stayed empty until you manually pressed
RENDER / REFRESH PREVIEW.

That made the Studio look like video playback was broken.

v1.5.1 changes the Studio review flow:

- the first clip automatically renders/loads when Studio opens
- clicking a clip automatically renders/loads that clip
- PREV / NEXT automatically load the selected clip
- GOOD + NEXT automatically loads the next clip
- switching carts automatically loads the first clip
- after committing a draft, the first clip of the new revision loads
- RENDER / REFRESH PREVIEW is renamed PLAY / REFRESH PREVIEW

Repair/crop/trim/replacement actions already rendered their changed preview
automatically and continue to do so.

The first time an uncached clip is opened, Studio may need a moment to encode
its browser-safe preview. After that, the cached preview should load quickly.
