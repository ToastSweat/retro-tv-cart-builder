RETRO TV CART BUILDER v1.1.3
==============================

AUTOMATIC CARTRIDGE VOLUME LABELS
---------------------------------
When building directly to a USB drive, the finalizer now renames the flash drive
to the cart's friendly channel/cart name before copying files.

Examples:
  NICK01 -> NICK1
  NICK02 -> NICK2
  CN01   -> CN1
  CN05   -> CN5
  BOOM01 -> BOOM1
  SNICK  -> SNICK
  FOX96  -> FOX96
  POWER  -> POWER

Usage:

  .\Build-RetroCartFinal.ps1 -CartID NICK01 -TargetDrive 'E:\'

The drive will be labeled NICK1 automatically.

You can override the label:

  .\Build-RetroCartFinal.ps1 `
      -CartID NICK01 `
      -TargetDrive 'E:\' `
      -VolumeLabel 'NICKTOON'

FAT/FAT32 labels are sanitized and capped at 11 characters.

This version also retains the v1.1.2 dual copy progress bars.
