RETRO TV CART BUILDER v1.3.3
==============================

SIGN-OFF RECOVERY / APPEND COMMAND
----------------------------------
If a cart revision was already created before its sign-off was registered, you
no longer need to re-apply an old review CSV just to append the sign-off.

After registering the sign-off and refreshing the asset registry:

  .\Add-RetroCartSignOff.ps1 -CartID NICK01

This creates one new immutable revision containing the exact current manifest
plus the configured SIGN_OFF at the very end.

Existing ReviewStatus, trims, crops, audio repairs, and source choices are
preserved exactly. The newly-added sign-off alone is UNREVIEWED.

Then:

  .\Build-RetroCartReview.ps1 -CartID NICK01
  .\Open-RetroCart.ps1 -CartID NICK01
