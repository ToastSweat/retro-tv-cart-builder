RETRO TV CART BUILDER v1.4.4
==============================

REPAIRABLE REPLACEMENTS + DURABLE EPISODE REPLACEMENT POOL

The NICK02 r002 preflight did exactly what v1.4.3 was supposed to do:
it refused to commit an incomplete revision.

It also exposed two follow-on usability problems.

1. A selected branding replacement could become NEEDS_CROP / NEEDS_CLIPPED /
   BAD_AUDIO elsewhere in the same review. Older builds then rejected the
   selected replacement and silently fell back to another READY asset.

2. Episode assets were primarily registered from cart configs. That meant a
   cart could have no spare READY episodes available when a SHOW needed
   replacement.

v1.4.4 fixes both.

REPAIRABLE REPLACEMENTS
-----------------------
A manually selected replacement may now be used when its source health is:
  NEEDS_CROP
  NEEDS_CLIPPED
  BAD_AUDIO

provided a valid repair recipe exists.

Recipe order:
  1. Repair instructions from another slot in the SAME exported review.
  2. Approved repair instructions from a prior FINAL build.

The replacement keeps those trim/crop/audio instructions in the new manifest
instead of being discarded or replaced by an unrelated asset.

Non-repairable states such as BAD_QUALITY, DUPLICATE, EXCLUDED, MAYBE, etc.
remain blocked.

HANDPICKED EPISODES
-------------------
New helper:
  Add-HandpickedEpisode.ps1

It writes durable replacement-pool episode records to:
  STATE\handpicked-episodes.csv

Initialize-RetroCartLibrary.ps1 now imports those episodes as UNIQUE assets,
so they can be used as SHOW replacements without adding them to a cart's
planned Episodes array.

This is the preferred way to maintain spare episode replacement candidates.
