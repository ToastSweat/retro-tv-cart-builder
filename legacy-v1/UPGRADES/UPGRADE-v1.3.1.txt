RETRO TV CART BUILDER v1.3.1
==============================

- GOOD clips are green in the sidebar and clip strip.
- Unchanged GOOD clips carry forward as GOOD to the next revision.
- Replaced/trimmed/cropped/new clips remain UNREVIEWED for repair verification.

HANDPICKED NICK SIGN-OFF
Recommended folder:
  F:\[VIDEOS]\Commercials\Bumpers-Handpicked\Bumpers\Nickelodeon\

Register it without rescanning 131GB:

  .\Add-HandpickedBranding.ps1 `
      -Path 'F:\[VIDEOS]\Commercials\Bumpers-Handpicked\Bumpers\Nickelodeon\YOUR SIGN OFF.mp4' `
      -Network NICK `
      -Type SIGN_OFF `
      -CartID NICK01

Then:
  .\Initialize-RetroCartLibrary.ps1

The helper durably records the clip in STATE\handpicked-branding.csv and writes
SignOffAssetID into config\NICK01.json.

Applying the current NICK01 review will append the sign-off to the next revision.

BAD_AUDIO + CROP/TRIM
BAD_AUDIO means replace the source. Its trim/crop values are not transferred to
the replacement. The apply script now warns if both are present.

Trim + crop DO combine when the source is being kept.

For NICK01 r003:

  .\Apply-RetroCartReview.ps1 `
      -CartID NICK01 `
      -ReviewCsv 'C:\path\to\NICK01-r003-review-state.csv' `
      -AutoReplace `
      -LeanBreaks

Then:
  .\Build-RetroCartReview.ps1 -CartID NICK01
  .\Open-RetroCart.ps1 -CartID NICK01

That produces r004, applies repairs/replacements, thins the old break clutter,
and appends the new Nick sign-off.
