RETRO TV CART BUILDER v1.2.1 HOTFIX
====================================

Windows PowerShell 5.1 was treating FFmpeg's normal cropdetect STDERR output as
a terminating NativeCommandError because the script uses
$ErrorActionPreference = 'Stop'.

v1.2.1 relaxes ErrorActionPreference only around the cropdetect probe, captures
the FFmpeg log, then restores the original setting.

No cache or NICK01 state needs to be deleted.

Replace Build-RetroCartReview.ps1 and rerun:

  .\Build-RetroCartReview.ps1 -CartID NICK01

To remove downloaded-script warnings for the toolkit:

  Get-ChildItem -LiteralPath 'F:\[VIDEOS]\Retro TV Cart Builder\Toolkit' -Recurse -File |
      Unblock-File
