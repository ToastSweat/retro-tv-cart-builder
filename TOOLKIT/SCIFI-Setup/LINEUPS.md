## Selection context

These are curated classic rerun selections, not a statistically proven ranking of the most frequently syndicated episodes. Archived Sci-Fi schedules support the channel/show pairing and include several selected titles. The December 2000 marathon lists Time Enough at Last, To Serve Man, Nightmare at 20,000 Feet, The Invaders, The Howling Man, The Monsters Are Due on Maple Street, Will the Real Martian Please Stand Up, The Masks, and others.
